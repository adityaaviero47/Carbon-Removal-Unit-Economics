# Farmer onboarding playbook: regen-ag and biochar programmes (draft SOP)

A draft operating procedure that turns the model's levers into field steps and weekly KPIs. It is a portfolio draft written from public sources, not a tested company SOP.

## Why these KPIs
The model shows soil-carbon margin is decided by MRV cost per acre and acres per field agent, biochar margin by delivery cost per tonne, and AFOLU credit value by days to registration. Each KPI below maps to one of those levers.

## Stages

| # | Stage | Owner | Exit check | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Village selection | Field lead | Cluster of 300+ eligible acres within one block; practice baseline under 20% adoption (VM0042 additionality) | Eligible acres per village |
| 2 | Awareness meeting | Field agent | Revenue share, payment timing and data use explained in local language | Attendance to enrolment rate |
| 3 | Enrolment and consent | Field agent | Signed agreement, land record, bank account for direct transfer | Onboarding cost per farmer (INR) |
| 4 | Plot mapping | Field agent + remote sensing | Polygon captured and matched to satellite boundary | Share of plots mapped first time |
| 5 | Baseline data | Field agent | 3-year practice history (tillage, residue, fertiliser, irrigation) logged | Data completeness (%) |
| 6 | Practice adoption | Agronomist | Practice evidence (photo, geotag, date) each season | Adoption rate by practice |
| 7 | Biochar feedstock (biochar only) | Logistics | Stalk collected within target radius, weight and moisture recorded | Feedstock cost per tonne, collection radius (km) |
| 8 | MRV and verification | MRV team | Monitoring report passes internal QA before the verifier | MRV cost per acre (USD), days to registration |
| 9 | Payout | Finance | Payment reaches the farmer's account; grievance channel open | Days from issuance to payout |

## Weekly dashboard (one row per project)
Farmers enrolled, acres enrolled, onboarding cost per farmer, data completeness, adoption rate, MRV cost per acre, biochar delivery cost per tonne vs USD 143 benchmark, days in registration, payout delay, grievances open.

## Red flags that stop a village
- Adoption already above 20% (credits would not be additional).
- Data completeness under 80% after two visits.
- Biochar feedstock radius that pushes delivery cost above the credit price.
